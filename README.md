# Good Governance Roadmap, 2082 (नेपाल सुशासन मार्गचित्र, २०८२)

This repository contains the official **Good Governance Roadmap, 2082** report published by the Government of Nepal. The document is provided in Markdown format for developers, researchers, and text‑based users who wish to read, process, or contribute to the open governance dialogue.

## 📄 Report Summary

The Office of the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers has publicly released the **Good Governance Roadmap, 2082**. The roadmap was prepared by a committee formed under the chairmanship of **Mr. Govinda Bahadur Karki**, Secretary at the Prime Minister’s Office.

- **Committee formation date:** 14 Poush 2082 (cabinet decision led by Sushila Karki)
- **Report submission date:** 3 Chaitra 2082
- **Public release date:** (Sunday – current government release)

The previous government did not publish the report despite its submission. The current administration has now made it available to the public.

## 👥 Committee Members

The committee comprised joint‑secretaries from various ministries, Gen‑Z representatives, and civil society members:

- **Coordinator:** Govinda Bahadur Karki
